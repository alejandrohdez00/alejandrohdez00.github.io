About

Hi! I’m Alejandro, an ELLIS PhD student at the Max Planck Institute for Human Development and the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, co-supervised by Iyad Rahwan and Bernhard Schölkopf.

I believe science and art are evolutionary processes, where ideas are mutated, shared and selected over time by a population of agents. My research therefore explores open-ended AI for cultural and scientific discovery. Inspired by cultural evolution, I develop methods that help AI systems continually explore original, coherent ideas in domains without clear reward signals, such as early-stage scientific ideation.

Outside research, I enjoy creative writing, literature, cinema, video games and opera.

I’m always happy to chat about open-endedness, creativity, or a good Borges story. Feel free to send me an email!
